Real recordings without injected faults

Every detector was also replayed over the untouched recordings, station by station. This is description, not accuracy: there are no labels, and the team data is in-sample for the rules. The clearest case is the development set's Station B, whose cameras delivered about half the frames its recorder logged from its first episode. The rules were in alert for {{ retrospective.team.station_b.rules.share_of_episodes_in_alert | pct0 }} of its episodes, in line with the recorder's own validity flag ({{ retrospective.team.station_b.recorder_flag.share_of_episodes_in_alert | pct0 }}). Robust z and the model compare a station with its own start, so this station looked normal to them: robust z raised no camera alert on it, and the models were in alert for at most {{ retrospective.team.station_b.model_303.share_of_episodes_in_alert | pct1 }} of its episodes.

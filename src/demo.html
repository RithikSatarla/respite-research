Interactive demo

Watch a station go wrong, episode by episode

Pick a scenario. Each one is twenty consecutive real episodes from one XDOF recording stream, either untouched or with a synthetic fault written into the files from a known episode onward. The charts show what Respite measured on each episode; the strips show what each detector said after every episode.

What you are looking at. These are stored records from the benchmark's external test set, replayed in your browser. The recordings are real; the faults are synthetic and labelled. Nothing here is live telemetry, and nothing is computed in your browser except the drawing.

How to read it

  • The shaded area marks the episodes the fault was written into. Intermittent faults hit only some of them.
  • A red cell means the detector was alerting after that episode. The text under each strip gives its first alert after the fault started and the reason it gave.
  • "Not measured in this format" means the recording does not carry what that chart needs. In this format, for example, frozen camera frames cannot be seen at all.
  • The trained model shown is the seed-101 model. It was trained on another team's recordings and mostly does not transfer to this recorder; the benchmark page has the numbers.
